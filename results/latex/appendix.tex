\section{Issues Found in the Original Phase-2 Code}
\label{app:bugs}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \texttt{test\_\allowbreak prompt\_\allowbreak effect.\allowbreak py} encodes images with \texttt{ClimateSAM.\allowbreak set\_\allowbreak infer\_\allowbreak img()}, which feeds the
  first three raw channels to the encoder and skips the learned input adapter (\texttt{encode\_\allowbreak images()} applies it).
  Effect: $-0.00$ to $-0.03$ IoU.
  \item The multi-scale generators predict three channels, of which only TC and AR are supervised (as independent
  sigmoids) by \texttt{compute\_\allowbreak generator\_\allowbreak loss}; the untrained background channel takes part in the final argmax.
  \item \texttt{LogisticRegressionPrompter} classifies the output of the first ViT block instead of the last.
  \item \texttt{StreamSegMetrics.\allowbreak update(gt, pred)} is called with swapped arguments in several scripts. IoU is
  symmetric and unaffected; mean and frequency-weighted accuracies are affected.
  \item All original Phase-2 training scripts select the best epoch on the test set.
  \item The official ClimateNet CG-Net checkpoint predicts background everywhere in evaluation mode, because its stored
  BatchNorm running statistics do not match its weights; it works with batch statistics.
  \item The fine-tuned CG-Net was trained on all 398 training images, so its outputs on training images are optimistic.
\end{enumerate}
